`Introduction` short and sweet today. Psalm 42:5 says:

Why are you cast down, O my soul,
    and why are you in turmoil within me?
Hope in God; for I shall again praise him,
    my salvation and my God.

Preacher D. Martyn-Lloyd Jones says, "Have you realized that most of your unhappiness in life is due to the fact that you are listening to yourself instead of talking to yourself?"

`Body`

As our family approaches a major fork in the road ahead, as we get closer to doing overseas work, hoping that it will work out, waiting on the Lord, I find myself overwhelmed. I want to just curl up into a blanket and escape the stresses of the world. Reading the above quote by Preacher Jones helped frame my current struggle. I am realizing instead of talking to myself I am listening to myself. Instead of taking on the feelings of being overwhelmed and allowing it to be my identity, I must, like the sons of Korah in Psalm 42, engage in self-talk. I here and now say to myself, "Why are you cast down? Hope in God; for I shall again praise him, my salvation and my God!" LORD God help this to be the song of my heart when I am overwhelmed. We have to remind ourselves to hope in God.

Help me to realize God that when I am cast down that is because my soul pants for you like a deer pants for flowing streams (Psalm 42:1). I need you, Yahweh. More of You.

As Paul says in 1 Corinthians 9:27, "...I discipline my body and keep in under control". We must subdue our minds, our souls, our bodies into submission for Jesus Messiah and through Jesus Messiah's power. He is the Promised One the ancient Israelites hoped for; like them He is the one that I hope for, every morning, for his new mercies (Lamentations 3:23). Because like the remnant among the ancient Israelites, God has helped me recognize that I am not enough. I cannot climb the mountain of the Lord, I cannot attain perfection. But Jesus Messiah can, and he did. He came down to the base of the mountain and carried me on His back to the summit.


`Conclusion`

My soul waits for the Lord
More than watchmen wait for dawn (Psalm 130:6)

I am waiting for you, Jesus, anxiously and excitedly. Come rescue me and my overwhelmed heart.
