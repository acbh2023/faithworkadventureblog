`INTRO`

Some __CONTEXT__: Why does it say, "***But*** standing there by the cross of Jesus were [Mary, Mary, Mary and John]"? ***But*** usually indicates contrast with a previous statement. What is the contrasting statement? (John 19:23-24)

Just before this, the soldiers that crucified Jesus were casting lots over who got to keep the crucified Son of God's tunic. Instead of beholding the cross, recognizing that the sinless Savior of the world was taking on the sins of the world like the three Marys and John were, these spiritually blind fools were playing games; they were gambling.

`MAIN BODY`

Why do I believe this text teaches us an important lesson about the "strange family of God?" Jesus passes the torch of his own sonship of Mary down to John, and the role of Mary's motherhood of Jesus to John, and then John takes Mary to his own home, indicating he is taking her in and taking care of her. So, in the kingdom family of God, we step up and take care of each other's mothers/parents when our brother's Kingdom duties supersedes his family duties. ***This is the opposite of how the world operates, only taking care of its own kind.*** So what is the family of God and why is it strange? The family of God is the body of believers. John the apostle was very focused on looking at the body of believers from a familial perspective in his writings, especially First John. We have been adopted into a new family, and so the question arises, what should this body of believers family look like? How should we operate? What should our focus be? This John 19:25-27 text gives insight into this question.

In this true story, the family of God is represented in these characters: __Mary__ (Jesus' mother), __Mary__ (of Clopas), __Mary__ called __Magdalene__, and John the Apostle, and most importantly, the firstborn son __Jesus__ (v. 25-26). Who are they and why is it important that they are here at the cross included among the family of God? As you read Please note that Mary means "rebellion" in Hebrew:
- __JESUS, KING OF THE JEWS__:
    - in total and complete agony, the God of all creation is totally humiliated and in agonizing, suffocating pain. It is in this state that he makes sure that someone takes good care of the well-being his mother and his brother, thinking nothing of His own well-being as he slowly suffocates on the cross.
    - _He is the only family member that deserves to belong. He is the older brother none of us deserve, laying his life down for his little brothers and sisters._
- __Mary__, the mother of Jesus (MMoJ):
    - She gives birth to the firstborn of Zion. Mary represents Zion. So when Jesus tells John to take care of his mother, He is not only telling John to take care of his literal mother, but this is also an instruction for John (and us) to take care of Zion, to take care of the church.
    - She is commanded by Jesus in this true story to take care of John like he is her own son. This reminds me of Matthew 25:31-46, the story of the sheep being separated from the goats. When Jesus is pleased with the sheep because they gave food to the hungry, water to the thirsty, shelter to the homeless, and clothes to the naked, Jesus says of these actions "Truly, I say to you, as you did it to one of the least of these, my brothers, you did it to me" (v. 40). Jesus here at the cross is commanding Mary (who represents the church) to take care of her neighbor as if she is taking care of her own son. He is commanding Mary (the church) to take care of her brother in Christ as if she is taking care of Christ himself. This command is for us, too!
    - _Mary loved the LORD from her youth and was probably raised in a strong (earthly) family of faith. The song she sings in Luke 1:46-56 is saturated with a knowledge of Old Testament scriptures. There is an important place in the family of God for those who have been faithful their whole lives and grew up in God-fearing homes._
    - 
- __Mary__, sister of MMoJ, wife of Clopas:
    - This seems to be Jesus' aunt and likely the mother of disciple James son of Alphaeus, not James son of Zebedee but the other, younger disciple James. What is interesting about this Mary being at the cross is that since she is identified as one among the children of God beholding and mourning the death of Jesus, as a believer should, since she is a believer more than likely her son James led her to following Jesus. Again ***This is backwards and strange to how the world operates.*** In the world, you give birth to children and pass on your legacy to them and that is what life is about. It is not that simple with the kingdom family of God. While it should be James' mother teaching him the ways of God, Jesus through James has reversed the curse of sin in James' family, bringing healing, restoration, and a legacy of repentance into his mothers life. _If Mary of Clopas belongs in the family of God, so do your earthly family members too, old and young. Reverse the curse in Jesus name and by His power._
    - Note: Since she is related to MMoJ, it is totally possible that she has been faithful all her life like MMoJ since they are from the same family.

- __Mary__ called __Magdalene__:
    - Luke 8:2 says this about Mary: "Mary, called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had gone out." Seven represents completion or totality. So the fact that she had seven demons is not to be overlooked. She had reached complete, utter, and maximal demonic occupation.
    - It appears that Jesus or the disciples may have called Mary (which means "rebellion") by a new name, Magdalene (which means "inhabitant of a tower"). Isn't it amazing that she went from being a completely demon-oppressed rebel to a tower inhabitant. Jesus had become her tower, her fortress. No longer was she caught up in the futile demonic rebellion against the undefeatable fortress of Christ, but she had entered into said fortress through the true, narrow gate of God, Christ himself, and was working for the King, demolishing the very demonic strongholds that use to hold her captive.
    - _If Mary called Magdalene, formerly totally consumed by demonic forces, belongs in the family of God, so does anyone with a dark, demon-oppressed background that has repented and turned to Christ._

- __John__ the apostle:
    - A former fisherman who left his nets and followed Jesus. Knew deeply and reminded himself often in His writings that we was beloved by Jesus. May we all often remind ourselves of this. May we, like John, as a part of the royal priesthood, take care of the church, which is represented her by Jesus' mother.
    - _There is a place in God's family for normal, blue collar workers like John. Also, as John was a teacher and an apostle with a blue collar background, so, contrary to the family of the world, the family of God does not require a studious, academic background in order to teach, preach and reach the lost as a leader.

_For more on the connection I just made between MMoJ and Zion_:
- __How is Zion equivalent to the church?__ The Book of Isaiah spends a lot of time fleshing this out; I recommend reading all the way through it in one sitting at least once in your life. An example verse that draws this connection between Zion and the church:
    - Isaiah 14:32b: The Lord has founded Zion, and in her the afflicted of his people find refuge.
- __How is Jesus the firstborn of Zion?__ Isaiah 28:16 says, "therefore thus says the Lord God, “Behold, I am the one who has laid as a foundation in Zion, a stone, a tested stone, a precious cornerstone, of a sure foundation: ‘Whoever believes will not be in haste.’"
- Check out Revelation 12 to see one example of how I draw a connection between Mary and how she represents Zion. The crown of twelve stars the woman in Revelation 12 represents the 12 tribes of Israel most likely, which points to the strong possibility that this woman is Zion.


`Conclusion`
This gives text us a picture of what kind of people the strange family of God is comprised of and what its focus should be
__The strange family of God is comprised of:__ lifelong faithfuls (MMoJ), newly faithfuls (Mary of Clopas), repentant demon-oppressed rebels (Magdalene), and everyday blue collar workers turned ministers of God (John). ALL TYPES OF BACKGROUNDS ARE WELCOME, INCLUDING YOU AND YOUR FAMILY MEMBERS!
__The strange family of God's focus as seen in the passage is:__ to spend time at standing at the cross (meditating on it, marveling at it, loving and thanking Jesus for it, etc.) and to take care of each other like family regardless of what earthly family you come from. _Even in the midst of suffering on the cross, Jesus is bringing into focus his key teaching of the greatest commandment from Matthew 22:37, 39: "You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind...and...You shall love your neighbor as yourself. On these two commandments depend all the Law and the Prophets.”_
