the pharisees of Jesus' day had the same Bible as Him; the pharisees of today have the same bible as us.

the pharisees of Jesus' time misunderstood the Old Testament (Ez 36:36, Ex 34:6, etc.) the pharisees of today misunderstand the New Testament. And just because someone can parrot good theology does not exempt them from being a pharisee.

look back at the parable of the good and bad seed because I think there is a lesson here that there is a Judas among every congregation, no matter how big or small, from a house church to a megachurch, from the pews to the pulpit. This is how our God designed church life to be, it seems (wait until harvest lest the good sprouts be removed with the bad).
