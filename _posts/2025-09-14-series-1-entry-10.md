__SOCIAL MEDIA AND FALSE TEACHING__

Every once in a while I make the mistake of hopping onto my wife's facebook and scrolling on facebook video reels. I made that mistake today. Facebook studies us as we interact with its platform, and gives us what it thinks we want to see. Facebook has identified me as a Christian according to its definition of a Christian and thus feeds me Christian content according to its definition of Christian content. Facebook is a secular organization and I have no interest in its definition of Christian content, and yet I get sucked in sometimes. I am not saying it is sinful to do so, I think it is helpful to see what conversations are going on in mass culture, but we must be on our guard because Facebook does not have the Holy Spirit and cannot discern between right teaching and false teaching when it throws Christian content at us. Be careful.

For example, I have seen Mark Driscoll pop up in my video reels often; Mark Driscoll has been disqualified from ministry by his own church(es) multiple times, and yet he continues to pop up in new places and resume operating as a spiritual leader. Bill Johnson of Bethel Church (and Bethel Music) pops up on my facebook reels feed too. Bill Johnson has had people dump gold dust into his church ventilation system during services so that gold dust in pumped into the air above the congregation in the sanctuary during service so that congregants will think that God is blessing or anointing the service. From my understanding, these guys are both false teachers. I am not an expert in this subject, but I believe it is important we are always trying to discern. A man named Justin Peters, whom I trust, has a YouTube channel that has been helpful in this area. He is not inerrant but he is helpful. The Holy Spirit ultimately helps us discern (1 John 4:1-6). The New Testament focuses HEAVILY on being wary of false teaching, and I did not focus on it a lot in the past and it caused me to be deceived in some very devastating ways. Thank You God my Shepherd that You leave the 99 sheep to find the _1_ like me.

__`PART TWO`__

__MY DREAM THIS WEEK: The only acceptable form of abortion in God's eyes__

_Intro_

Is there an acceptable form of abortion in God's eyes? Yes, but it is not what you think (If you are panicking, jump to the last paragraph of this _Intro_ section to get the answer). I was talking with a coworker the other day, a self-labelled conservative, and he spoke very negatively of abortion, but then he went on to say that he thinks abortion is okay if someone has been sexually assaulted. I disagree wholeheartedly with making any exceptions like that and I believe God would disagree and He shows us that in His word. To clarify, I am NOT saying abortion is unforgivable. All sin is forgivable except an eternal rejection of God's offer for salvation. Abortion is forgivable! Jesus wants to forgive us. Anyways, oftentimes on this topic, Psalm 139:13 is brought up:

`For you formed my inward parts; you knitted me together in my mother’s womb.`

This verse is important for many reasons: it helps us know we were fashioned and formed by the __Best Artist of All Time__ and therefore we are a beautiful artwork of His, no matter how we feel or what the enemy tells us. But what does this have to do with whether someone who's been assaulted can have an abortion or not? A verse like Psalm 139:13 helps us see that God is ultimately in charge of forming someone, knitting them together, which means it is God who decides if a baby that is the result of sexual assault is successfully conceived or not. I would have never been born if someone in my family line a few generations ago would have decided that abortion was okay in the case of sexual assault. And yet God has turned that evil into good, creating a legacy of my father and mother loving Jesus and serving Him, and now God has brought me unto Himself and saved me so that I may teach transgressors His ways and sinners will return to Him too. 
Praise God. As Joseph said to His brothers, providing for them during a famine all those years later after they had sold him into slavery, "You meant evil against me, but God meant it for good." (Genesis 50:20a). God, who gives all free will (Revelation 2:20-21), and desires all to be saved (1 Tim 2:4), has the right as King of the Cosmos to go about achieving His ultimate plan of giving Himself glory, rescuing His people, restoring creation, and destroying evil however He pleases. This means that even evil things that happen fit under the umbrella of God's good and perfect plan.

There are some obvious objections to some of the things I just said:

Objection 1: _Some would describe God's ultimate plan of self-glorification as a selfish plan._ God is infinitely beyond the most wonderful, beautiful and loving being imaginable; Him being exalted above all, given all the glory is the best case scenario for us to be able to enjoy existence to the maximum degree. It is the best case scenario for not only Him but all creation. His beauty is unimaginably mind blowing. Jesus in his Kingly form had a power and beauty about Him that was so magnificent that it caused John to fall "at [Jesus's] feet as though dead." (Revelation 2:12-17). Jesus, God Himself, is described as having eyes like a flame of fire. Have you ever sat around a campfire and just watched the flames? They are entrancing, dancing about, infinitely unique with every flicker of the flame. That gives us just a little glimpse into the powerful beauty of Jesus' eyes (source: Dr. Donald S. Whitney).

Objection 2: _How could God throw people into hell? That is terrible._ It is in fact, terrible. It is true that verses like Romans 9:21 indicate that God chooses who goes to heaven and who goes to hell. But it is also true that God gives man free will. For example, of Jezebel in Revelation 2:20-21, a woman clearly destined for hell, Jesus says this: "I gave her time to repent, but she refuses." How can two things that seem contradictory be true at the same time? The Bible is full of many of these situations and they are called antinomies. Life is full of antinomies. How can life be so beautiful and wonderful like when my son and daughter were born, and yet so sad and tragic like all the children in Gaza that have died? This is the reality of life the way God designed it.

So what form of abortion is acceptable in the eyes of God? __Spiritual abortion.__ John Owen says, "Be killing sin, or sin will be killing you." The Psalmist of Psalm 137 expresses a similar sentiment toward Babylonian babies in Psalm 137:9 when they write, "Blessed shall he be who takes your little ones and dashes them against the rock!" While the Psalmist is likely talking about the physical babies of the physical empire of Babylon, Jesus shows us that all the Old Testament is about Himself in Luke 24:27, and so there is a spiritual, ultimate, and Christ-centered focus to Psalm 137:9 as well, since Jesus is ultimately the author. So how does this relate to us? James 1:14-15 for starters gives a very graphic depiction of how this relates to us in talking about how sin works:

`But each person is tempted when he is lured and enticed by his own desire. Then desire when it has conceived gives birth to sin, and sin when it is fully grown brings forth death.`

James uses the word "conceived" here, which is a biological term. Conception occurs only through a certain biological function between two parties. Considering that graphic image James gives us it should help us have a deeper understanding of the disgustingness of our sin. Read the verse against with the process of sex and childbirth in mind. I truly believe that is the image James is evoking here. Like the daughers of men were lured by the sons of God in Genesis 6 and begat the evil Nephillim, so our desire lures us in and impregnates our heart with the seed of sin, begetting sin which brings forth death.

I believe our job is to dash our spiritual sin babies against the rocks just like in Psalm 137:9. They are the result of our spiritual fornication and whoring after other gods like the book of Hosea and Ezekiel 16. We must kill our spiritual sin children at all costs, begging God for help in the task, or they will kill us like it says at the end of James 1:15, Whatever it takes to get sin out of you life, do it. _WHATEVER IT TAKES._ Paul talks about attaining resurrection from the dead "by any means possible" (Philippians 3:11)

_The Dream_

So what does all that intro have to do with my dream?

Remember Joel 2:28 says,
`“And it shall come to pass afterward, that I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh; your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, and your young men shall see visions.` God communicates with us in these ways these days, but we must be discerning to make sure they are in line with scripture.

A couple nights ago I fell asleep way too early when I should have stayed up doing homework. As I chose to disobey the Holy Spirit's nudging me to stay awake and do some homework, I fell asleep and had a very vivid, memorable dream. I was watching a man's actions like he was someone else, but then I realized I was watching myself, and being warned and instructed. This is graphic, but important and I believe it is a dream the LORD allowed me to have to warn me. At the start of the dream, the man is lured into being intimate with a forbidden woman. Then, all of the sudden, mid-intimacy, the woman transforms into a normal-looking, tan, strong man. Immediately the man's appearance began to shift again and the man shrinks slightly in size, no longer big and buff but smaller and his skin bright white with red warrior paint on his face. Next, very quickly and suddenly a bright white-skinned baby with red face paint is birthed. Immediately the baby was taken and smashed against the hard floor, killed and obliterated. And so the dream was over.

I believe this dream was a warning to me, a depiction of the aforementioned James 1:14-15, with a reminder of the antidote to the poison of sin, the antidote being the dashing of our evil sin babies against the rocks discussed in Psalm 137:9. Once again, in the words of John Owen, "Be killing sin, or sin will be killing you."

Jesus help us to be watchful, to be careful and help me to kill sin but only by Your power, Your leadership, and Your sword King Jesus. I am weak and You are strong.
